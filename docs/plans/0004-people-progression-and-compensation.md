## Problem Essence

A skill matrix does not explain advancement or authorize pay changes. Conflating eligibility, appointment and payment creates misleading employee promises and unsafe payroll behavior.

## Desired Outcome

Employees can understand applicable criteria and challenge results; authorized decision-makers govern rewards with effective-dated evidence and payroll acknowledgement.

## Ownership and Dependencies

Own progression paths, approved incentive policy, eligibility explanations, review/appeal and award decision history. Consume verified Skills evidence (0002) and workforce/payroll capabilities (0001). Do not implement payroll calculation or duplicate assessment authority. 0005 owns shared presentation; this workstream owns the business meanings shown there.

## Design Decisions

Recommend explainable eligibility followed by reviewed awards, rather than immediate automatic salary changes. Automatic entitlement is possible only under explicit approved policy and proven payroll controls. Keep appointments and proficiency incentives distinct. Policy discovery may proceed alongside training design; eligibility implementation waits for agreed assessment/version contracts.

### Progression product requirements

An employee should be able to see their applicable published position/tier profile; current verified standing; missing, expired or pending evidence; available advancement paths; required skill levels/certifications and other approved criteria; recommended actions and training; relevant published incentive or salary linkage; evaluation date and policy version; and how to request assessment, correction or review.

HODs propose position standards, evidence expectations, paths and incentive mappings. Recommended governance is HR review and authorized compensation publication before salary-linked policy becomes an employee promise. The owner must confirm that division of authority; do not silently replace HOD accountability with HR authoring all technical standards.

Model skill attainment, qualification for a role, appointment to a position, incentive eligibility, compensation approval and payroll execution as distinct decisions. A path may concern proficiency pay without a vacant position, or promotion requiring an appointment. Name these differences in the employee explanation.

Eligibility should retain a snapshot of the policy version, evaluated evidence, qualifying and unmet criteria, evaluation time and explanation. Review decisions retain approver, reason, effective date and any exception authority. If actual pay is represented, keep currency, pay period/basis, recurring versus one-off treatment and effective dates explicit; payroll owns calculation and statutory treatment.

Default recommendation: publish criteria, compute explainable eligibility, and require an authorized award decision. Automatic entitlements require separately approved policy, exact trigger and retroactivity rules, duplicate prevention, payroll acknowledgement and compensation/legal validation. Expiry or reassessment must not automatically reduce salary or claw back awards without an approved rule and lawful process.

Define treatment of policy changes while training or review is underway, grandfathering, expired certifications, transfers, partial completion, assessor conflicts, appeals and overturned assessments. Keep employee-visible reasons sufficient to challenge a result without disclosing other employees' records or confidential HR deliberations. Notifications must distinguish “eligible for review” from “pay increase approved” and “effective in payroll.”
